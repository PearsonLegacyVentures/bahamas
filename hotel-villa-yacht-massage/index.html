For Nassau hotels, vacation rentals, villas and yachts, Tranquilitas works around your setting so you can enjoy a private massage without an unnecessary trip across the island.

Hotels and resorts

Private in-room massage is best arranged where hotel policy permits outside providers. Some properties may require advance concierge approval or prohibit external treatments entirely. We ask guests to confirm the rules before reserving a therapist.

The booking request should include your hotel and general area, with detailed access information shared privately after confirmation. We don't promise access to individual resorts simply because they are in our service area.

Villas and vacation rentals

Villas often provide enough room for a massage table and can be convenient for couples or small groups. Check for a clean, quiet area, practical parking or entry instructions, and enough space to walk safely around the table.

When multiple guests want treatments, share the guest count, whether sessions can run sequentially, and any preferred schedule. Group arrangements require confirmation based on therapist availability.

Yachts and marina requests

For yacht massage, provide the marina or anchorage access plan, vessel setting and suitable treatment space. The team will discuss guest movement and arrival logistics before confirming. Not every onboard environment can safely accommodate a portable table.

Privacy, safety and clear communications matter more than decorative extras. We prefer confirming a realistic setup over promising an experience that will be difficult to deliver.

Booking without a second trip

The live Tranquilitas menu remains the pricing reference for treatment durations and service inclusions. The Bahamas hub gives you the local details, a direct request form and one team contact for arrangements.

Can I get a massage at my villa in Nassau?

Yes, if the Nassau or Paradise Island villa is a suitable and permitted private treatment space and a qualified local therapist is available. Confirm the address, property manager's consent, equipment access and your preferred treatment.

Can I get a massage at my villa in Nassau?

Yes, Tranquilitas accepts mobile massage enquiries for eligible Nassau and Paradise Island villas and vacation rentals. Provide the exact property, host approval, desired treatment, number of guests and time. Staff and travel are confirmed before a booking is accepted.

Are mobile spa services available on Paradise Island?

Tranquilitas can review mobile massage requests for approved Paradise Island hotel rooms, resorts and villas. Each property controls outside practitioner access, so availability is confirmed for the specific location and date.

Can I request sports massage on Paradise Island?

You can enquire about sports-style or focused massage for an eligible private property on Paradise Island. The precise treatment, professional suitability, venue permission and scheduling must be confirmed.
