Frequently asked questions

Can you send therapists to any yacht?

No. We first verify the port, captain's permission, stable treatment space, safe boarding and provider availability. An anchored yacht may need an approved tender transfer.

Can guests request the same experience in other countries?

Yes. Share the complete itinerary and dates; our team will explore each stop separately. Coverage and local regulations vary, so the service is confirmed destination by destination.
