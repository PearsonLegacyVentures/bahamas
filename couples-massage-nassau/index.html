A couples massage gives two people a shared pause, whether you choose the Tranquilitas treatment room, a private Nassau accommodation or a carefully arranged waterfront setting.

Choose the experience before the setting

A couples treatment is typically arranged side by side with two therapists, matching each guest's preferred pressure and the available time. It is suitable for an anniversary, honeymoon, milestone or an ordinary afternoon that deserves more attention.

If you prefer a dedicated room with fewer logistics, an in-spa couples appointment is the simplest choice. For an in-room or villa experience, we coordinate access, two tables and enough space for the therapists to work safely.

In-spa, mobile, or near the water?

The Collins Avenue spa provides a controlled indoor environment. Private mobile couples sessions can suit hotel suites and villas where the property permits outside treatments. Beachside settings need more planning: private or approved shoreline access, shade, wind, weather and privacy all matter.

If you are booking for a special occasion, tell the team what you want the atmosphere to feel like. Additional decorations or setup requests should be discussed before confirmation; they are not silently included.

Live options, not copied pricing

Our original Tranquilitas menu contains individual couples services with their current durations and rates. Because treatment lengths and inclusions vary, we send guests directly to that live menu rather than duplicating prices that might conflict later.

Share whether you prefer Swedish or deep tissue, where you are staying and the date. We can advise on a suitable combination and confirm the appointment details.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT
  • Have your preferred date and venue ready.
  • Allow space for two treatment tables if booking at your stay.
  • Ask about add-on setups or beach conditions before paying.

Good to know

Can we have different massage pressures?

Yes, tell the team each person's preferences in advance so your therapists can tailor the treatments.

Can couples massage come to our Airbnb?

Yes, where the accommodation and available space are suitable and the property allows it.

Is a beach couples massage the same as the in-spa treatment?

No. A beachfront treatment involves additional location, weather, access, privacy and setup considerations. Confirm the specific service when booking.
