A real spa, in the middle of Nassau

Tranquilitas Day Spa operates at #7 Fourth Terrace, Collins Avenue. This is our established in-person location, not a pop-up or another online booking directory. Guests can arrange massage appointments, selected facial services and packages through our original Tranquilitas booking system.

The in-spa setting suits guests who want a dedicated treatment room rather than turning a hotel bedroom into a massage space. Nassau residents and travelers can both choose this option. A confirmed time is required before arrival.

Choose the treatment that fits your body

Swedish massage is a popular choice when you want to ease into the experience. Deep tissue focuses on firmer pressure and stubborn muscular tension. Couples sessions create room for two people to share a treatment without needing a mobile setup.

Facials, specialty experiences and package durations vary. Rather than copying a second rate sheet that can become outdated, we link directly to the live Tranquilitas menu for current inclusions, pricing, durations and booking times.

Getting here and what to bring

Our spa is on Collins Avenue in Nassau. We recommend checking your route and allowing travel time, particularly around downtown traffic or scheduled cruise activities. If you need directions, contact our team before your appointment.

Wear what is comfortable. If you have allergies, pressure preferences, recent injuries or mobility considerations, mention them when booking so the team can prepare appropriately.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT
  • Book an appointment before arriving.
  • Use the live booking menu for accurate total pricing and duration.
  • Tell the team about accessibility or treatment preferences in advance.

Yes, select couples sessions are available. Confirm your preferred duration and time when booking.
