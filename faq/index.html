NASSAU MASSAGE FAQ

Clear answers.

Where is Tranquilitas Day Spa located?

We are at #7 Fourth Terrace, Collins Avenue, Nassau, The Bahamas. It is the business's existing physical spa.

Is the Bahamas site another Tranquilitas location?

No. This is an additional Tranquilitas Bahamas website for the same established spa and mobile service, not a second physical location.

Can I book mobile massage at a hotel?

Yes, where the hotel or resort permits outside providers. Let us know the property and confirm access before booking.

Can therapists come to Paradise Island?

Private mobile massage can be requested for suitable Paradise Island accommodation, subject to access, scheduling and therapist availability.

Is a beach massage available in Nassau?

Yes, Tranquilitas offers beach massage. A suitable private or approved beachfront setting and weather-dependent setup must be confirmed first.

Can we get a couples massage?

Yes, selected two-person treatments are offered in-spa and in suitable mobile settings. See the live menu for current options.

Do you provide equipment for mobile sessions?

Our mobile team brings core treatment equipment, including a massage table, linens and oils. Additional arrangements depend on the service.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Ask the team directly. Same-day requests depend on the date, venue and therapist availability; displayed online lead times may differ.

Are transportation and beach extras included?

Do not assume they are. Confirm transport, venue access, setup, decorations and any additional fees before payment.

How do I find the latest prices?

The original Tranquilitas Wix booking menu is the live source for current service pricing and availability. The Bahamas site deliberately does not duplicate a separate rate schedule.

Will a request automatically confirm my booking?

No. A WhatsApp or email enquiry is only a request until our booking team explicitly confirms the appointment and any payment instructions.

Can I book during a Nassau cruise stop?

Possibly. Check your ship's all-aboard deadline and allow travel time to and from the spa before choosing a slot.

Can we book a group event?

You can enquire about groups and corporate treatments. We will confirm the available therapist capacity and practical details for your location.

Are the reviews on this site from the same business?

Yes. We link to existing Google and Setmore guest feedback for Tranquilitas Day Spa, the same physical Nassau spa featured here.
